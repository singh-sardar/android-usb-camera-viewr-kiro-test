## 🎯 Feature Highlights

- ✅ Full-screen camera display
- ✅ Multiple resolution support (4K to VGA)
- ✅ Frame rate control (60 to 15 FPS)
- ✅ Image transformations (rotation, flip)
- ✅ Settings persistence
- ✅ Auto-launch on camera attach
- ✅ Camera controls (brightness, contrast, etc.)
- ✅ Auto-retry mechanism
- ✅ USB hotplug support

## 📊 System Requirements

- **Android**: 7.0+ (API 24+)
- **USB Host**: Required
- **Camera**: UVC-compliant
- **Permissions**: Camera, USB access
